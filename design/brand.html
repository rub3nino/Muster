Muster Brand guidelinesLogo

Calm, exact, on your side

Muster is the back office of an event that matters to people. It should feel like a quiet notebook, not a dashboard shouting numbers. Grey canvas, white sheets, one dark ink, one violet for “you are here”.

Principles

01One next step

Every registration shows the single action that moves it forward. Everything else is one click away, never in the way.

02Nothing goes live by accident

What visitors see is drafted, reviewed as a list of changes, then published. Destructive actions always ask, and say what happens next.

03Colour means something

Violet is selection and focus only. Green, amber, red and blue live inside chips and callouts, never as decoration.

04Phone first, for real

Organisers work from a phone at the check-in desk. Tables turn into cards, drawers into sheets, targets are 44 px.

05Three languages, same tone

English, Italian and Romanian. Short sentences, verbs on buttons, no jargon. Dates in Italian time, always.

06Private by default

Health and faith data are labelled where they appear, never in logs, never in read-only exports.

Colour

Tokens in css/tokens.css. Dark mode redefines the same names.

Type

System sans (SF Pro on Apple, Segoe on Windows, Roboto on Android). No web font to load, no layout shift.
